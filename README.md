## 🎮 Overview

Welcome to **Among-Us-Trainer**, a meticulously crafted companion utility designed for Windows users who want to explore the many layers of social deduction gameplay with a personalized, configurable control set. This project was born from a simple idea: every player deserves to shape their experience in ways that feel natural, intuitive, and entirely their own.

Whether you're a seasoned crewmate navigating the treacherous corridors of the Skeld, or a mischievous impostor orchestrating the perfect alibi, Among-Us-Trainer offers a suite of adjustable gameplay controls powered by a flexible hotkey system. Think of it as your personal mission control panel — a cockpit where every toggle, slider, and macro is tuned to your fingertips.

This repository is not just another tool; it's a philosophy. We believe that customization is the heartbeat of a great gaming experience. Our trainers are built with transparency, user agency, and ethical play in mind, giving you the reins to experiment in private lobbies, practice sessions, and solo exploration.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

Our team has poured countless hours into making Among-Us-Trainer a robust, responsive, and rewarding utility. Below is a curated breakdown of what makes this project stand out from the crowd.

### 🧭 Responsive User Interface
The interface adapts fluidly to different screen resolutions and Windows scaling settings. Whether you're on a compact laptop display or a sprawling ultrawide monitor, every panel, slider, and toggle snaps into place with pixel-perfect precision. The layout reflows gracefully, ensuring no control is ever out of reach.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
Communication is at the heart of Among Us, and so it is with our tool. Among-Us-Trainer ships with translation files for a growing list of languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Mandarin. Community contributions are warmly welcomed, making the trainer accessible to a truly global audience.

### 🕒 24/7 Customer Support
Questions at 3 AM? We've got you covered. Our support desk operates around the clock, staffed by real humans who understand the nuances of the tool. Whether it's a configuration hiccup or a feature request, our team responds with empathy and speed.

### ⌨️ Fully Configurable Hotkeys
Rebind any action to any key combination you prefer. From toggling movement modifiers to cycling through vision modes, every hotkey is yours to define. Profiles can be saved, exported, and shared, letting you switch between "practice mode," "speedrun mode," and "cinematic mode" with a single click.

### 🧩 Modular Control Panel
The control panel is organized into collapsible modules — Movement, Vision, Tasks, Sabotage Simulation, and Cosmetics Preview. Each module can be enabled or disabled independently, keeping the interface clean and focused on what matters to you.

### 🔄 Real-Time State Persistence
Your settings are stored securely and restored instantly every time you launch the application. No more re-configuring from scratch; the trainer remembers your preferences as faithfully as a loyal crewmate.

### 🛡️ Sandboxed Practice Environment
Experiment freely in a controlled sandbox that never touches public matchmaking. This is your personal holodeck — a place to test, learn, and refine without impacting the experience of others.

### 🎨 Theme Customization
Choose from a palette of carefully designed themes, including Midnight, Aurora, Retro Arcade, and Minimalist White. Each theme adjusts contrast, accent colors, and typography for optimal readability.

### 📊 Session Analytics Dashboard
Track your practice sessions with lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics. View heatmaps of movement, task completion ratios, and reaction times — all stored locally on your machine.

### 🔔 Smart Notifications
Discreet toast notifications inform you when a toggle is activated, a profile is loaded, or a hotkey conflict is detected. Never lose track of your configuration state again.

## 📦 What's Inside the Package

The distributed archive contains a carefully arranged set of components, each serving a distinct purpose:

- **Launcher executable** — a lightweight entry point that validates your environment and unpacks the control panel.
- **Configuration directory** — housing JSON profiles, hotkey maps, and theme files.
- **Localization pack** — language files in a human-readable format, editable with any text editor.
- **Documentation folder** — quick-start guides, FAQ sheets, and troubleshooting checklists.
- **Asset library** — icons, sound cues, and UI resources.

No external dependencies are required. The utility is self-contained and portable, meaning you can carry it on a USB drive and use it on any compatible Windows machine.

## 🛠️ Getting Started Without the Fuss

We've deliberately avoided conventional command-line setup rituals. Instead, our onboarding flow is visual and guided:

1. Retrieve the package using the indicator placed at the bottom of this document.
2. Extract the archive to a folder of your choosing.
3. Launch the primary executable.
4. Follow the on-screen wizard, which walks you through language selection, hotkey presets, and theme choice.
5. Press the default activation chord to summon the control panel in-game.

That's it. No terminal incantations, no dependency wrangling — just a smooth, welcoming entry.

## 🧭 Hotkey Reference Table

| Action | Default Binding | Notes |
|--------|-----------------|-------|
| Toggle Control Panel | F8 | Rebindable |
| Cycle Movement Profile | F9 | Profiles: Walk, Sprint, Glide |
| Toggle Vision Overlay | F10 | Adjustable radius |
| Open Task Simulator | F11 | Sandbox only |
| Switch Theme | F12 | Cycles installed themes |
| Panic Hide | Ctrl+Shift+H | Instantly conceals the overlay |
| Reload Configuration | Ctrl+R | Applies latest profile changes |

Every binding can be reassigned through the Settings menu, and conflicts are highlighted in real time.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Among-Us-Trainer is built on three pillars: **agency**, **clarity**, and **respect**. Agency means you control every aspect of the experience. Clarity means the interface never obscures what it's doing. Respect means we never interfere with other players' enjoyment in public lobbies.

We see the trainer as a sketchbook, not a shortcut. It's a place to doodle with mechanics, understand the underlying systems, and appreciate the craftsmanship that goes into a well-balanced multiplayer title.

## 🔐 Privacy and Data Handling

All session data remains on your local machine. We do not transmit telemetry, analytics, or personal identifiers to remote servers. The trainer operates fully offline once installed, making it suitable for air-gapped environments and privacy-conscious users.
